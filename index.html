Multiscale Climate Dynamics Group

Our research combines physics-based modeling and artificial intelligence to investigate the drivers and dynamics of multi-scale moisture transport. We analyze how turbulent and convective processes shape large-scale atmospheric circulation, and how global thermodynamic constraints govern localized physics. We develop process-based diagnostic frameworks that translate complex scale interactions into assessments of climate risk, continuously evaluated against high-spatiotemporal campaign observations.
